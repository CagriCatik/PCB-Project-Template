ESP32 DevKit Carrier

KiCad 10.0.7 blank carrier with functional review rules, socket-pin inventory and optional ESP32-S3 firmware scaffold.

Select and verify your exact DevKit revision, memory variant, header geometry and power architecture first. No socket, pin assignment or validated circuit is preselected.

Firmware uses PlatformIO/Arduino with a documented N8 baseline. Other variants require reviewed build settings. GPIO output is disabled until explicitly assigned.
