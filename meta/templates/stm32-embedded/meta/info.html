STM32 Embedded (PCB-Project-Template)

Blank KiCad 10.0.7 board with optional STM32 firmware infrastructure.

  • firmware/CMakePresets.json: host unit tests and an STM32 target preset
  • Platform-independent firmware/app module behind a BSP interface
  • STM32CubeMX output plugs in at firmware/bsp/stm32/cubemx; no MCU or pinout is preselected

Note: a template is a starting point, not a validated design. ERC/DRC results and exports are tooling checks; electrical, thermal and fabrication readiness require review and prototype validation.
