Arduino Shield (PCB-Project-Template)

Arduino Uno Rev3 shield based on KiCad's official Arduino Uno Shield template (outline, headers and mounting holes).

  • Base design: (c) 2021 Caleb Reister, (c) 2015 Jonathan Iapicco, KiCad libraries license (CC-BY-SA 4.0 with design exception)
  • Legacy KiCad 9 VRML model references migrated to the KiCad 10 STEP models where they exist
  • Optional PlatformIO firmware (firmware/platformio.ini) with host unit tests

Note: a template is a starting point, not a validated design. ERC/DRC results and exports are tooling checks; electrical, thermal and fabrication readiness require review and prototype validation.
