Generic PCB (PCB-Project-Template)

Microcontroller-independent KiCad 10.0.7 project with a blank schematic and board generated by KiCad itself.

  • hardware/, firmware/, components/, manufacturing/, docs/, qa/ project layout
  • Verification gates in qa/verification.json
  • No circuit, footprints or 3D models are preselected

Note: a template is a starting point, not a validated design. ERC/DRC results and exports are tooling checks; electrical, thermal and fabrication readiness require review and prototype validation.
